## Notes

- TV run has no back and no boxes. The 2x4 rails ARE the top rear member: screw them to
  the studs, hang the dividers off them (notched), and the bottom ladder carries the
  floor load on the kick sleepers. Nothing squares the run except the wall, so shim the
  rails plumb and straight before anything else goes on.
- Wall screws: #10 x 4". The stack to the stud is now rail 1-1/2 + drywall 1/2 = 2",
  leaving 2" of stud bite (no back panel behind the TV rails any more). Same screw in the
  tall cabinet where the stack is rail 1-1/2 + hardboard 1/8 + drywall 1/2 = 2-1/8.
- Top-front stringers are SIX separate pieces between the dividers, so the dividers are
  notched only at the rear for the rail. Pocket-screw or toe-screw each stringer end into
  the divider. The bottom stringers and the three bottoms are jointed under dividers 2
  and 4 (joint centred under the divider, cross block directly below).
- Doors: 1/8 reveal between doors and under the slab, 1/4 at the right wall and at the
  tall cabinet. Every TV door hinges on the divider to its left and opens right. Door 1
  hinges on the tall cabinet's right side: its hinge edge sits 1/4 inside that face
  rather than covering it. Screw a 5/8 packer 11 x 3 (spruce or MDF offcut) to
  that face and use a half-overlay
  hinge on the packer (edge lands at 18-41/64, matching the model's 18-5/8), or use an
  inset-crank hinge with no packer. Doors 2 to 6 use half-overlay hinges (9 mm cover) on
  the 1/2 dividers with plate screws no longer than 12 mm (15 mm stock screws break
  through 1/2 ply). The tall door also takes half-overlay hinges: its edge is 1/4 off the
  wall on a 5/8 side, a 3/8 cover. So all 17 hinges are one SKU, half overlay, 0 mm plates.
- Tall door and every 5/8 MDF door: 5/8 MDF bows if one face is primed and the other left
  bare overnight. Prime both faces in the same session and store flat.
- 1/2 spruce dividers are 12-1/2 tall between the bottom and the slab; the rail takes the
  rear 1-1/2 of the top 3-1/2, so nothing deeper than 13-7/8 stands full height at the back.
- Kicks are 5/8 MDF on 2x2 sleeper blocks behind them (board #8), shimmed to the floor;
  no adjustable legs or kick clips. The TV kick is recessed 2-1/2, the tall kick flush.
- Carcass depths: tall = 24-7/16 - 1/8 back - 5/8 door = 23-11/16; TV = 16 - 5/8 door =
  15-3/8 (the wall is the back).
- Rail notch in each divider: 3-1/2 up x 1-1/2 in at the rear top corner, cut in the model
  with the rail bodies as the tool so it tracks `rail_t` and `rail_h`. Removed volume per
  divider: 2-5/8 cubic inches. Hand saw and chisel in the real build.
- Plane the rounded factory arris off the rear face of the tall cabinet rails so the
  hardboard back nails flat. Sight every 2x4 for bow and use the straightest three for the
  TV rails; a bowed rail pushes the whole run out of line.
