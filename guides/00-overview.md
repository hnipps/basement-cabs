## The design in one paragraph

Four frameless plywood boxes: one tall cabinet (18-3/8 W x 82-11/16 H x 24-7/16 D)
and three identical TV boxes (60-7/16 W x 14-1/4 H x 16 D) sitting side by side to
the right wall. All boxes stand on adjustable legs so their undersides are 4-15/16
above the floor, with a kick plate in front of the legs. A single 3/4 MDF top slab
sits on the three TV boxes and brings the TV run to 15 high, flush with the wall
panelling at 19-15/16. MDF slab doors overlay every box front so no plywood edge
shows. Hanging rails inside each box are screwed to the studs. Everything is painted
black.

## Coordinate words used throughout

- **Front**: the door side. **Rear**: the wall side.
- **Left / right**: as you stand in the room facing the doors. Tall cabinet is on the left.
- **Side**: a vertical box panel. **Partition**: the vertical panel in the middle of a TV box.
- **Rail**: the 1-1/2 x 3-1/2 solid lumber (2x4) strip inside the top rear of a box,
  for wall screws. The tall cabinet has a second one on top of its bottom panel.
