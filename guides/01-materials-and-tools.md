# 1. Materials and tools

## Sheet goods

| Material | Buy | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| 3/4" plywood, 4x8, cabinet grade (birch or maple faced) | 5 | box sides, tops, bottoms, partitions, shelves, kick |
| 3/4" MDF, 4x8 | 3 | 7 doors, top slab (2 pieces), tall cabinet right side |
| 1/4" plywood, 4x8 | 2 | backs (1 sheet is enough with zero mistakes) |

Buy plywood flat and check each sheet for bow before it goes in the car. Keep MDF dry;
one wet corner and the panel is scrap.

## Lumber already on hand

| Material | Have | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| 2x4 (1-1/2 x 3-1/2 actual): 8' x2, 6-1/2' x2, 47" x1 | 5 boards | All 5 hanging rails. Cut plan in `../CUTLIST.md` |
| 2x2 (1-1/2 x 1-1/2 actual): 8' x1 | 1 board | No part in the design. Cauls and spacer sticks |

Nothing to buy here. The rails used to be plywood; 2x4 comes at exactly 3-1/2" wide
already, which removes five long narrow rips, and a 1-1/2 thick rail takes a wall
screw far better than a 3/4 plywood edge. Sight each board for bow and use the two
straightest for the 58-15/16 rails. Let them sit indoors a few days first if they came
off an outdoor rack.

## Tools you have that this build uses

Circular saw with a straightedge guide, miter saw, two drills, clamps (you need at
least four 24" bar clamps and two 36" or longer), tape, 4' level, square, chisels,
block plane, sanding block.

## Tools to borrow or improvise

- **Straightedge guide for the circular saw**: an 8' factory edge of 1/4 plywood
  screwed to a wider base. Build it first, guide 2 explains.
- **Brad nailer**: optional. Backs can be screwed.
- **Laser level**: rentable for a day, makes the install much faster. A 4' level and
  a long straight board also work.
