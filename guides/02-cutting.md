# 2. Cutting

Goal: every panel in `../CUTLIST.md` cut, labelled in pencil on a hidden face, and
stacked by box. One day. Do not cut the doors or the top slab pieces to final size
yet; rough them 1/2" oversize and finish them in guide 4 and guide 7 against the
real installed boxes.

## Before the first cut: measure the wall again

Measure the rear wall width at 5" and at 20" above the floor, and at the front edge
of where the cabinets will be. If any reading is below 199-11/16, open the Fusion
model, set `wall_w` to the smallest reading minus 1/4, and regenerate the cut list.
The doors and slab hide a small gap at the right wall; a box that is too long cannot
be hidden.

## Build the saw guide

1. Rip a 12" wide strip off a sheet of 1/4 plywood, keeping the factory edge. This is
   the fence. Rip a 3" strip off the same sheet; this is the base.
2. Glue and screw the fence on top of the base, factory edge outward, with about 2"
   of base showing on the cut side.
3. Run the saw along the fence once; the saw trims the base to exactly its own
   offset. From now on the base edge is the cut line. Clamp it to the mark and cut.

Use a fresh 40-tooth blade for plywood and MDF. Score MDF first with a shallow pass
if the face chips.

## Cutting order

Rip long strips first, then cross-cut on the miter saw or with the guide. Cut the
biggest pieces from each sheet first. Sizes below are finished sizes; leave the pencil
line on the waste side and measure from the same tape all day.

### 3/4" plywood

**Sheet A: tall cabinet.**
Rip a 23-7/16 strip the full 96" length. Cross-cut the tall left side, 82-11/16 long.
From the remaining 24-3/8 wide strip cross-cut five 16-7/8 pieces, then rip two of
them to 23-7/16 (top and bottom) and three to 22-11/16 (shelves).

**Sheets B and C: TV box tops, bottoms, sides.**
Each sheet: rip three 15" strips. From each strip cut one 58-15/16 top or bottom,
then two 14-1/4 sides from the 37" left. Two sheets give 6 tops and bottoms and 12
sides; you need 6 sides, so use 3 of the extras for partitions (rip to 12-3/4) and
keep the rest as spares.

**Sheet D: shelves.**
Rip three 15" strips. Cross-cut six 29-3/32 TV shelves (two per strip). From the
third strip take the 4th tall shelf, 22-11/16 x 16-7/8 after ripping. The remaining
3" of sheet width and the offcuts are spare. The rails come out of 2x4 instead, so
there is no narrow rip to make here.

**Sheet E: kick.**
Rip 4-15/16 strips. Kick TV: three pieces totalling 181-5/16, cut so the joints land
behind the box seams at 60-7/16 and 120-7/8 from the tall cabinet. Kick tall:
18-3/8. Kick return: 10-15/16. The rest of the sheet is spare.

### 3/4" MDF

**Sheet 1.** Rip 23-7/16 for the tall right side and 18-1/8 for the tall door, both
82-11/16 long. Leave the door 1/2 over in both directions until guide 4.

**Sheet 2.** Rip a 16" strip the full 96" length: top slab piece 1. Rip two 14-1/8
strips: three doors each at 30-1/32, again 1/2 over for now.

**Sheet 3.** Rip a 16" strip and cut it to 85-5/16 plus 1/2: top slab piece 2. The
rest is spare for a door you miss-cut.

### 1/4" plywood

**Sheet 1.** Tall back 82-11/16 x 18-3/8. From the remaining 29-1/2 strip, two TV
backs 60-7/16 x 14-1/4.
**Sheet 2.** Third TV back. Keep the rest for shelf-pin templates and paint test
panels.

### Solid lumber (2x4)

Five crosscuts on the miter saw, no rips. Square one end of each board first, then
measure every length from that end.

| board | cut | leftover |
|---|---|---|
| 8' #1 | TV rail 58-15/16 | 37" |
| 8' #2 | TV rail 58-15/16 | 37" |
| 6-1/2' #1 | TV rail 58-15/16 | 19" |
| 47" | both tall rails 16-7/8 | 13" |
| 6-1/2' #2 | untouched | 78" |

Then run the block plane down both long edges of each rail's rear face to take the
rounded factory arris off, so the 1/4 back nails flat against it.

Keep the 78" board and the offcuts. They earn their space later: shims at the wall in
guide 6, bearers under the sheets while you break them down with the circular saw, and
cauls for the slab glue-up in guide 7.

## Labelling

Write the part name from the cut list on the inside face of every piece with a
pencil, plus an arrow pointing to the front edge. Stack tall cabinet parts, TV box
parts, doors, and backs in four piles. Sand the cut edges lightly with 120 to remove
splinters; no more than that.
