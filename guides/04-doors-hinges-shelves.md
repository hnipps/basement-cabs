# 4. Doors, hinges, shelf pins

Half a day. Do this after the boxes are built and before painting, then take the
doors back off for paint. Hinges are set up once and re-hung after finishing.

## Door sizes

Trim the rough-cut MDF doors to final size now.

| Door | Final size | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| Tall door | 82-11/16 x 18-1/8 | 1 |
| TV doors | 30-1/32 x 14-1/8 | 6 |

TV doors are full-height overlay: bottom edge flush with the box bottom, top edge
1/8 below the underside of the slab. Reveal between neighbouring doors is 1/8.

If the installed wall width differs from the model, recut the six TV doors to
(actual width of the TV run minus 2 x 1/4 end gaps minus 5 x 1/8 reveals) divided by
6. That keeps every reveal equal.

## Which edge gets the hinges

Every door hinges on a box outer side, and every pair opens from the partition like
a pair of French doors. So all hinges are the same type: full overlay, 0 mm plate.

| Door | Hinge edge | Plate mounts on |
|---|---|---|
| Tall door | left (wall side) | tall cabinet left side |
| TV door 1 | left | box 1 left side |
| TV door 2 | right | box 1 right side |
| TV door 3 | left | box 2 left side |
| TV door 4 | right | box 2 right side |
| TV door 5 | left | box 3 left side |
| TV door 6 | right | box 3 right side |

The 1/4 end gap at the left wall and at the tall cabinet side is the swing
clearance for the hinge edge. Use 110° hinges, not 155° or 170°, which throw the
edge out further.

## Hinge cups

35 mm Forstner, 1/2" deep (13 mm), in the back face of the door.

- Cup centre 7/8" (22.5 mm) in from the hinge edge.
- TV doors: two cups, each 3-1/2" from the top and bottom edges.
- Tall door: five cups, at 3-1/2" from top and bottom and three spaced evenly between
  (roughly every 19").

Mark, punch the centre, drill with the door clamped face-down over scrap. Go slow;
MDF burns if you force the bit. Press the hinge in, square it to the door edge, and
drive the two cup screws.

## Mounting plates

Plates go on the inside face of the side the door hinges from.

- Front of the plate holes: 37 mm (1-15/32) back from the front edge of the side.
  A Kreg or Rockler concealed-hinge jig does this in one go, or make a scrap-plywood
  template with two 5 mm holes 32 mm apart and 37 mm from a fence.
- Height: match each cup. For TV doors, cup centre 3-1/2" from the door top means the
  plate centre is 3-1/2 down from the top of the door, which sits 1/8 below the box
  top. So plates at 3-5/8 and 10-5/8 from the top edge of the side.
- Tall door: door bottom flush with cabinet bottom, so plates at 3-1/2 from the
  bottom and 3-1/2 from the top, and the three between to match the cups.

Clip the doors on, then adjust: the screw nearest the door moves it in and out, the
plate slot moves it up and down, the rear screw changes overlay. Get every reveal to
1/8 with a 1/8 spacer between doors. Then unclip the doors for painting; the hinge
arms stay set.

## Shelf pins (tall cabinet)

5 mm holes, 32 mm apart, in two rows on each side.

- Front row 37 mm (1-15/32) from the front edge of the side, rear row 37 mm from the
  rear edge of the carcass (the back panel is nailed over it, so measure from the
  plywood edge, not the back).
- Start 6" above the cabinet bottom, stop 6" below the top. Keep these margins: the
  rails are 1-1/2 thick solid lumber and occupy the top 3-1/2 and bottom 3-1/2 of the
  cabinet, and a shelf is 22-11/16 deep, which clears a 3/4 rail but not a 1-1/2 one.
  The 6" margins keep every shelf position clear of both rails. Extend the pin rows
  toward either end and the shelf will foul a rail.
- Depth 1/2". Put tape on the bit or use a stop collar. Through-holes in the MDF
  right side would show on the exposed face.

Use a shelf-pin jig, or make one: a 1/4 plywood strip with holes drilled on a 32 mm
pitch, clamped against the front edge. Flip the same jig for the rear row so the rows
match.

Shelves are 22-11/16 wide for a 22-7/8 opening; the 3/32 slack lets them drop in.
Start with 4 shelves; add more later if you want.

## TV shelves

Fixed, on pins or on pocket screws through the box sides and partition. Pins are
simpler: one row of four holes per bay at 7" up, 37 mm from front and rear.
