# 4. Doors, hinges, shelf pins

Half a day. Cups, plates and pin holes are drilled before painting, with the parts
flat on the bench. Doors are test-hung on the tall box now, painted in guide 5, and
hung for good in guide 6.

## Door sizes

Trim the rough-cut MDF doors to final size now, tape on the line.

| Door | Final size | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| Tall door | 82-11/16 x 18-1/8 | 1 |
| TV doors 1 to 6 | 29-63/64 x 14-3/8 | 6 |

TV doors: bottom edge flush with the front stringer underside (4-15/16 off the
floor), top edge 1/8 under the slab. Reveal 1/8 between doors, 1/4 at the tall
cabinet and at the right wall. The tall door is flush with the exposed right side and
1/4 off the left wall.

If the installed TV run is not 181 wide, recut the six doors to (actual width
minus 2 x 1/4 minus 5 x 1/8) divided by 6, and move the dividers to match (guide 3
step 8: each divider's left face is door width + 1/8 + 9 mm - 1/2 to the right of the
previous door's left edge).

## Which edge gets the hinges

Every TV door hinges on the divider to its left and opens to the right. The tall
door hinges at the wall.

| Door | Hinge edge | Plate mounts on | Hinge |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tall door | left (wall side) | tall left side, inner face | half overlay, 5 |
| TV door 1 | left | tall right side, outer face, on a 5/8 packer | half overlay, 2 |
| TV doors 2 to 6 | left | right face of dividers 1 to 5 | half overlay, 2 each |

All 17 hinges are the same SKU: 110°, half overlay (9 mm cover), soft-close, 0 mm
plate. 110° not 155° or 170°, which throw the hinge edge out further.

Why: the tall door's edge sits 1/4 from the wall on a 5/8 side, so the door covers
3/8 (9.5 mm) of the side, a half-overlay cover. Doors 2 to 6 cover 9 mm of a 1/2
divider, leaving 1/8 plus a hair for the neighbour. Door 1's hinge edge sits 1/4
INSIDE the tall cabinet's right face (the face carries on 8-7/16 past the door), so
no hinge on that face lands the door: glue and screw a 5/8 packer (spruce offcut,
12-1/2 tall x 3 wide) to the tall side's outer face at the plate height, 37 mm zone,
and mount the plates on the packer. Half overlay on the packer puts the edge 1/64
right of the model; the hinge adjustment eats that. The alternative in `CUTLIST.md`,
an inset-crank hinge straight on the face, needs a 6 mm inset cover; check the
maker's chart before buying it. A full overlay hinge on the 5/8 packer puts the
edge flush with the face with no swing gap; do not use that pairing.

## Hinge cups

35 mm Forstner, 12.5 mm deep in 5/8 MDF (that leaves 3 mm; a depth stop, not tape),
in the back face of the door.

- Cup centre 7/8" (22.5 mm) in from the hinge edge.
- TV doors: two cups, 3-1/2 from the top and bottom edges.
- Tall door: five cups at 3-1/2, 22-7/16, 41-3/8, 60-1/4 and 79-3/16 from the bottom
  edge (3-1/2 in from each end, three evenly between, every 18-15/16).

Mark, punch the centre, drill with the door clamped face-down over scrap. Go slow;
MDF burns if you force the bit. Press the hinge in, square it to the door edge, drive
the two cup screws.

## Mounting plates

Plates go 37 mm (1-15/32) from the plate holes back to the front edge of the panel,
32 mm between the two holes, 5 mm holes. A Kreg or Rockler concealed-hinge jig does
this in one go, or make a template from the 1/2 MDF offcut: two 5 mm holes 32 mm
apart, 37 mm from a fence.

| Where | Reference | Plate centres |
|---|---|---|
| Tall left side, inner face | front edge; heights from the side's bottom edge | 3-1/2, 22-7/16, 41-3/8, 60-1/4, 79-3/16 |
| Dividers 1 to 5, right face | front edge; heights from the divider's bottom edge (the divider stands on the bottom, 2" above the door bottom) | 1-1/2 and 8-7/8 |
| Tall right side, on the packer | the TV door plane, 15-3/8 from the wall, so holes at 13-29/32 from the wall; heights from the tall cabinet's bottom edge | 3-1/2 and 10-7/8 |

Plate screws into the 1/2 dividers: the 15 mm screws in the hinge box break through
a 12.7 mm panel. Use 5 x 12 mm Euro screws or #6 x 1/2 wood screws, pilot 2 mm, and
add a dab of glue under each plate. Into 5/8 spruce and the packer the stock screws
are fine.

## Test hang

Clip the tall door on, then adjust: the screw nearest the door moves it in and out,
the plate slot moves it up and down, the rear screw changes cover. Set 1/4 to the
wall side and flush at the right. Unclip for painting; the arms stay set. TV doors
can only be test-hung on the wall (guide 6); check each divider's plates with a door
on the bench instead: clip the door on a divider lying flat and confirm the door
edge lands 9 mm onto the divider.

## Hang (during guide 6)

Doors 1 to 6 in order from the tall cabinet. Clip each on, set the reveal to 1/8
with a spacer against the previous door, bottom edge flush with the stringer
underside. Door 6 should finish 1/4 off the right wall; if it is more or less the
dividers are off, not the door. Open every door fully: nothing touches the slab
underside, the neighbour, or the wall.

## Shelf pins (tall cabinet)

5 mm holes, 32 mm apart, two rows on each side, drilled before assembly (guide 3).

- Front row 37 mm (1-15/32) from the front edge of the side, rear row 37 mm from the
  rear edge of the panel (the hardboard back is nailed over it later).
- Start 6" above the cabinet bottom, stop 6" below the top. The rails are 1-1/2 thick
  and fill the top 3-1/2 and bottom 3-1/2 at the rear; a shelf is 23-1/16 deep and
  runs to the back, so it fouls a rail if a pin row reaches that far.
- Depth 1/2. Stop collar on the bit. A through-hole in the MDF right side shows on
  the exposed face.
- Skip any pin position within 1" of a hinge plate height (22-7/16, 41-3/8, 60-1/4
  from the bottom): the plate and arm stand 5/8 into the cabinet in the shelf's
  path. If a shelf must sit there, notch its front-left corner 1-1/4 deep x 2-1/2
  long.

Shelf-pin jig, or a shop-made one: a hardboard strip with holes on a 32 mm pitch,
clamped against the front edge. Flip the same jig for the rear row so the rows match.

Three shelves, 23-1/16 x 17-1/16 after the 1/16 trim in guide 2, four pins each.

## TV bays

No shelves: the bays are 12-1/2 tall and 15-3/8 deep, and the rail takes the rear
1-1/2 of the top 3-1/2. Nothing deeper than 13-7/8 stands full height at the back.
