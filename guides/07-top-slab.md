# 7. Top slab

Half a day, plus drying. The slab is two pieces of 3/4 MDF, 16 deep, that become one
after filling and painting.

## Fit

1. Piece 1 is 96 long. Piece 2 is cut to make the total match the actual installed
   TV run: measure from the tall cabinet's right side to the right wall at the box top,
   subtract 96, cut piece 2 to that. Cut the joint end of both pieces square and clean
   on the same saw setting.
2. Dry fit. The joint lands at 96 from the tall cabinet, which is over box 2 (box 2
   spans 60-7/16 to 120-7/8), so the joint is fully supported by a box top. The slab
   front edge is flush with the door faces; the rear edge touches the wall. If the
   wall is not straight, scribe the rear edge with a block plane rather than leaving a
   gap at the front.

## Fix

3. Screws from below: from inside each box, pilot up through the box top and drive
   1-1/4 screws into the slab, 6 per box, 1" from the front and rear edges. The
   screws go 1/2 into the slab; do not use anything longer or they come through the
   top. Glue on the box tops helps and stops squeaks.
4. At the joint: glue the two end faces with wood glue, pull the pieces together, and
   screw both pieces to the box top within 3" of the joint. Clamp a straight board
   across the joint on top while it dries so both faces stay coplanar. Wipe squeeze-out.

## Hide the seam

5. Next day, fill the seam with 2-part filler, spread with a wide spreader, slightly
   proud. Also fill any dents.
6. Sand flat with 220 on a block, running your hand across the joint in the dark or
   with a torch held low. If you can feel it, fill again.
7. Prime the whole top face and front edge. Sand 220. Second primer coat on the seam
   area if you can still see a line.
8. Two colour coats, same paint as the doors. Let it cure a week before loading it.

## If the seam shows later

MDF moves little, but a hairline can open after a season. Fill with paintable
caulk, wipe flush, touch up with a small brush. Eggshell or matte paint makes this
invisible; gloss does not.
