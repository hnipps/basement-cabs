# 3. Tall box and ladder

Two jobs in one guide because they use the same joinery. The tall box is built in the
shop now (half a day). The TV ladder is built on the wall during installation, after
painting; guide 6 tells you when to come back here.

## Joinery

- Tall box: glued butt joints held with pocket screws from the inside face. Jig at
  the 5/8 setting, 1" coarse screws, glue on every joint. Three pockets per 17-1/8
  joint, first one 1-1/2 from an edge.
- Tall rails: 1-1/2 thick 2x4. Jig at the 1-1/2 setting, 2-1/2" coarse screws, two
  pockets per end.
- Pockets go in the ends of the top, bottom and rails, screwing into the sides. Never
  drill pockets in the sides. Keep pockets on the top and bottom 2" clear of the
  front-left corner where the hinge plates land (guide 4).
- Ladder: #8 x 2-1/2 construction screws through 2x2 into 2x2, two per joint, glue on
  the cross blocks. Dividers: 1" pocket screws at the 1/2 setting from the divider
  face into the bottom and into the top-front stringer, pockets on the hinge-plate
  side where the door hides them.

## Tall cabinet (shop)

Parts: left side (5/8 spruce) and right side (5/8 MDF) 82-11/16 x 23-11/16; top and
bottom 23-11/16 x 17-1/8; two rails 17-1/8 x 3-1/2 (2x4); back 82-11/16 x 18-3/8
hardboard. Shelves (23-1/16 x 17-1/8) stay out until guide 4.

1. Drill the shelf-pin holes in both sides now, flat on the bench (guide 4, "Shelf
   pins"). Drill the hinge-plate holes in the left side too (guide 4, "Mounting
   plates"); the plate template registers off the front edge.
2. Drill pockets in both ends of the top, bottom and both rails.
3. Lay the left side flat, inside face up. Glue and screw the bottom on its end,
   flush with the front and rear edges. Glue and screw the top the same way at the
   far end.
4. Rails: the top rail tight under the top at the rear, the bottom rail on the bottom
   at the rear, planed faces to the rear, flush with the rear edges. Glue and screw
   their ends into the left side.
5. Stand the assembly up and add the MDF right side. Screws into MDF: pilot 3/32,
   drive gently, stop when snug. MDF strips if you keep going. That side is the one
   exposed face of the whole unit; keep it on cardboard, face down, never on the floor.
6. Measure both diagonals across the front. Equal means square.
7. Lay the hardboard back on the rear, flush on all four edges (it covers the rear
   edges of the sides, top and bottom: 18-3/8 wide, 82-11/16 tall). Nail or screw
   every 6" into the sides, top, bottom and both rails. The back locks the box square;
   check the diagonals once more before the last nails.

Box outside: 18-3/8 W x 82-11/16 H x 23-13/16 D with the back on. The door adds
5/8 for 24-7/16. Fill screw heads that will show, wipe glue while wet: dried glue
shows through paint as a shiny patch.

## TV ladder (on the wall, during guide 6)

Everything is painted, the tall cabinet is fixed to its studs, studs are marked, the
floor high spot is known. Heights are above the finished floor at the high spot;
elsewhere the sleepers get shims.

| line | height | what sits on it |
|---|---|---|
| A | 4-15/16 | underside of both bottom stringers and the tall cabinet |
| B | 6-7/16 | top of the bottom stringers; bottoms sit on this |
| C | 6-15/16 | top of the 1/2 bottoms; dividers stand here |
| D | 15-15/16 | bottom edge of the 2x4 rails |
| E | 19-7/16 | top of the rails, dividers and top-front stringers; slab underside |
| F | 19-15/16 | slab top = panelling bottom edge |

1. **Lines.** Level line E on the rear wall from the tall cabinet to the right wall
   (laser, or a 4' level walked along a straight board). Mark line A and line B off it
   with a tape, not the floor.
2. **Rails to studs.** Three 2x4 rails 60-7/16 end to end, top edge on line E, butt
   joints at 60-7/16 and 120-7/8 from the tall cabinet (78-13/16 and 139-1/4 from
   the left wall); both joints hide inside dividers 2 and 4. Pilot and drive one #10
   x 4" into every stud, alternating high and low on the rail. Stack to the stud is
   rail 1-1/2 + drywall 1/2 = 2", so 2" of screw is in the stud. Stretch a string
   along the rail faces and shim behind the rails until the run is straight within
   1/16; a bowed wall pushes the whole run out, and nothing else squares it. Sight
   down the rail line before the last screws.
3. **Rear sleepers.** 2x2 blocks stood on end under line A, one under each cross
   block position (left end, the five divider centres at 30-1/2, 60-11/16, 90-13/16,
   120-31/32, 151-1/8 from the tall cabinet, and the right end), tight to the wall.
   Cut them 4-3/4 and shim to line A.
4. **Bottom rear stringer.** Three pieces 60-21/32, 60-5/16, 60-21/64 end to end on
   the sleepers, top on line B, joints at 60-11/16 and 120-31/32 from the tall
   cabinet (under dividers 2 and 4). Screw it to the studs with #10 x 4" and down
   into each sleeper with a #8 x 2-1/2. Level it end to end; this is the datum for
   the whole bottom.
5. **Cross blocks.** Seven at 12-3/8, glued and screwed through the rear stringer into
   their ends (two #8 x 2-1/2 each): one against the tall cabinet's right side, one
   centred under each divider position, one at the right wall.
6. **Front sleepers and front stringer.** Front sleepers under the front end of each
   cross block, with their front face 12-7/8 from the wall (that is where the kick
   screws to them; the kick is recessed 2-1/2 behind the door face). Screw the front
   stringer pieces to the block ends with the same three joints, front face 15-3/8
   from the wall, top on line B, level across to the rear stringer at every block.
   Check: 15-3/8 wall-to-front-face at every block, straight along the string, level
   both ways. Fix it now; the doors will show any error.
7. **Bottoms.** Three 1/2 spruce panels 15-3/8 deep, rear edge to the wall (scribe if
   the wall wanders), joints centred over cross blocks 2 and 4 (under dividers 2 and
   4). #8 x 1-1/4 every 12" into the stringers, and two into each block.
8. **Dividers.** Left faces at 30-17/64, 60-27/64, 90-37/64, 120-47/64, 150-57/64
   from the tall cabinet's right side (CUTLIST.md gives X from the left wall). Set
   each one on the bottom, notch over the rail, front edge flush with the front
   stringer face, plumb both ways. Two #8 x 1-1/4 through the divider into the rail
   face inside the notch, then 1" pocket screws down into the bottom (jig at 1/2).
   Dividers 2 and 4 each cover a rail joint; screw into both rail ends.
9. **Top-front stringers.** Cut each of the six to fit its bay: measure between
   dividers at the top front (nominal 30-17/64, four at 29-21/32, 29-59/64). Top on
   line E, front face flush with the divider fronts. 1" pocket screws from the
   divider face into the stringer ends. Bay 1's left end: screw a 2x2 offcut block to
   the tall cabinet's right side with #8 x 1-1/4 (pilot; 1/2 bite in 5/8 MDF) and
   screw the stringer to the block. Bay 6's right end: block to the right wall the
   same way, into a stud if there is one, drywall anchors if not; the slab bears on
   it, the rail carries the load.
10. **Check** every bay: divider plumb, top edges on line E, front faces in one
    plane along a string, 15-3/8 from the wall. Then the doors (guide 4, "Hang"), the
    kicks (guide 6), and the slab (guide 7).
