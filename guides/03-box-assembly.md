# 3. Box assembly

Goal: one tall box and three identical TV boxes, square, with backs and rails on.
One day. Work on a flat floor with a sheet of cardboard under the parts.

## Joinery

Every joint is a glued butt joint held with pocket screws from the inside face, so
nothing shows on the outside.

- Jig set for 3/4 stock. Screws 1-1/4" coarse. Glue on every joint.
- Exception, the rails: they are 1-1/2 thick 2x4, not 3/4 plywood. Reset the jig to
  1-1/2 stock and use 2-1/2" coarse screws for their ends, two pockets per end.
- Drill pocket holes in the ends of the tops, bottoms, partitions, and rails. The
  screws go into the sides. Never drill pockets in the sides themselves.
- Three pockets per 15" joint, five per 23" joint, spaced evenly, first one 1-1/2"
  from an edge.
- Keep pockets 2" away from any position that gets a hinge plate (guide 4 gives the
  positions: 3-1/2" from top and bottom of each door edge). Pockets on tops and
  bottoms are on the inside face, so they never conflict.

## TV box (make three)

Parts per box: 2 sides 15 x 14-1/4, top and bottom 58-15/16 x 15, partition
15 x 12-3/4, rail 58-15/16 x 3-1/2 (2x4, 1-1/2 thick), back 60-7/16 x 14-1/4.

1. Drill pockets: both ends of the top and bottom (into the sides), both ends of the
   partition (into top and bottom), both ends of the rail (into the sides).
2. Mark the partition centre line on the inside of the top and bottom: 29-3/32 from
   the left inside face, then a second line 3/4 further. Mark the rail line 3-1/2 down
   from the top on the inside of each side, at the rear edge.
3. Stand the two sides on their rear edges. Glue and screw the bottom between them,
   flush at front and rear. Clamp, check the front edges are flush with a finger, then
   screw.
4. Glue and screw the top the same way.
5. Notch the rear top corner of the partition 3-1/2 up by 1-1/2 in, so the rail can
   run past it in one piece. Hand saw down both shoulders, chisel out the waste; it is
   hidden, so it only has to clear, not look good. Then slide the partition in on its
   lines, flush at the front, glue, screw into top and bottom.
6. Glue and screw the rail between the sides, flush with the rear edge, tight up under
   the top. Its 1-1/2 thickness reaches 3/4 further into the box than a plywood rail
   would; that band sits above the shelf, so nothing fouls.
7. Measure both diagonals across the front. Equal means square. If not, push the long
   corner until they match.
8. Lay the back on the rear, flush on all four edges, and nail or screw every 6" into
   sides, top, bottom, partition, and rail. The back locks the box square. Check the
   diagonals once more before the last nails.

Sides are 14-1/4 tall and tops and bottoms sit between them, so the box is 14-1/4
high and 60-7/16 wide outside. Front edges of sides, top, bottom, and partition are
all in one plane: that plane is where the doors close.

## Tall cabinet (make one)

Parts: left side (plywood) and right side (MDF) 82-11/16 x 23-7/16, top and bottom
23-7/16 x 16-7/8, two rails 16-7/8 x 3-1/2 (2x4, 1-1/2 thick), back 82-11/16 x 18-3/8. Shelves stay out
until guide 4.

1. Drill the shelf-pin holes in both sides now, while the panels are flat (guide 4,
   section "Shelf pins"). Much easier than inside an assembled box.
2. Drill pockets in the ends of top, bottom, and both rails.
3. Lay the left side flat, inside face up. Glue and screw the bottom on its end, flush
   with the front and rear edges. Glue and screw the top the same way at the far end.
4. Stand the assembly up and add the right side. Screws into MDF: drive gently, stop
   when snug, MDF strips if you keep going.
5. Rails: one tight under the top at the rear, one on top of the bottom at the rear.
6. Diagonals, then the back, nailed every 6".

The MDF right side is the one exposed face of the whole unit. Keep it unmarked.
Any pocket screws you drove into it from the top and bottom are inside the cabinet.

## Before painting

Fill any screw heads on faces that will show. Wipe glue squeeze-out while wet with a
damp rag; dried glue shows through paint as a shiny patch.
