# 5. Finishing

Three to four sessions over a week, mostly waiting for paint to cure. Paint before
install: inside faces are unreachable once the boxes are in, and doors paint far
better flat on sawhorses than hanging.

## What gets painted

| Part | Faces | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Boxes | inside faces, front edges | Outside faces are hidden against walls, doors, or each other. Paint them anyway if you have paint left; a coat of primer is enough |
| Tall cabinet right side | outer face, front edge | The exposed MDF face. Best finish of the job goes here |
| Doors | both faces, all edges | Back face too, or MDF drinks moisture and bows |
| Top slab | top face, front edge | Underside gets primer only. Final coats after the seam is filled, guide 7 |
| Kick plates | front face | |
| Shelves | all faces and edges | |

## MDF prep

MDF edges are fuzzy and soak up paint. Seal every cut edge first: one coat of shellac
primer (Zinsser BIN) brushed on the edges only, dry in 30 minutes, sand flat with 220.
Alternatively wipe the edges with wood glue thinned 1:1 with water, let dry, sand.
Faces need nothing but a wipe.

## Plywood prep

Sand cut edges to 150. Fill any voids in the plywood edge with filler. Do not try to
sand grain out of plywood faces; it does not work. Those faces are hidden.

## Schedule

**Session 1: primer.** High-build primer on everything, rolled with a foam roller,
edges brushed. Let it dry overnight. Sand with 220 until smooth to the hand. Vacuum
and wipe with a damp cloth.

**Session 2: primer, second coat on MDF only.** The right side, doors, and slab.
Overnight, sand 220.

**Session 3: first colour coat.** Foam roller for faces, brush for edges, thin coats.
Water-based enamel self-levels; do not go back over a wet area. Overnight.

**Session 4: second colour coat.** Light 220 scuff first. Two coats is the target.
Third coat on the doors and the right side if you can see roller texture in low light.

Cure: water-based enamel is dry in hours and hard in about a week. Handle boxes after
a day, hang doors after three days, put objects on the slab after a week.

## Room and tools

Paint indoors in the room the cabinets go in, or a garage above 50°F. Doors on
sawhorses on painter's pyramids so both faces can be done in one session. Label the
back of every door with its number in pencil before priming; the label survives under
paint on the back face.
