# 5. Finishing

Three to four sessions over a week, mostly waiting for paint to cure. Paint before
install: the ladder is built from painted parts, and doors paint far better flat on
sawhorses than hanging. Only the slab's top face and the seam are painted in place
(guide 7).

## MDF first

MDF is every face you will see, and 5/8 MDF bows if one face is primed and the
other left bare overnight. So the first session is MDF only, both faces of every
piece, same session, stored flat afterwards.

| Part | Faces | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 7 doors | both faces, all edges | number on the back in pencil first; it survives under paint |
| Tall right side | outer face and front edge to finish; inner face one coat of primer | the one exposed face of the unit, best finish of the job |
| Top slab, 2 pieces | underside one coat of primer; top face and front edge primed only | colour goes on after the seam is filled, guide 7 |
| Kicks, 3 pieces | front face and top edge to finish, back primed | |

Seal every cut MDF edge before the first coat: brush the 1-2-3 primer on the edges,
let it dry, sand flat with 220, then prime the faces. If an edge still fuzzes after
that, one coat of BIN shellac from a quart on the edges only. Faces need nothing but
a wipe.

## Spruce behind the doors

Spruce sheathing is knotty and rough; all of it is behind a door. Sand cut edges to
150, fill voids in the edges, sand the faces with 120 to knock the fuzz off and stop.
One coat of primer and one of colour is enough inside.

| Part | Faces |
|---|---|
| Tall carcass inside: left side, top, bottom, back (hardboard) | inside faces and front edges |
| 3 shelves | all faces and edges |
| 3 TV bottoms | top face and front edge |
| 5 dividers | both faces and the front edge; leave the notch bare |
| Tall left side outer face | one coat of primer; it faces the wall |

The 2x2 ladder and the 2x4 rails are not painted. The top-front stringers show
through the 1/8 reveal under the slab from a low angle: brush black on their front
faces after they are up.

## Paint

One gallon of Zinsser 1-2-3 primer does everything above. One gallon of Behr Premium
Plus black eggshell does two coats on the MDF and one on the spruce. Eggshell, not
satin or gloss: it hides the slab seam and the roller texture.

## Schedule

**Session 1: prime the MDF.** Doors on painter's pyramids, both faces, edges brushed
first. Right side, slab pieces, kicks. Foam roller. Overnight, then 220 until smooth
to the hand, vacuum, damp wipe.

**Session 2: prime the spruce, second primer on MDF faces.** Everything in the spruce
table gets one coat. Doors and right side get their second primer. Overnight, 220.

**Session 3: first colour coat, everything.** Foam roller for faces, brush for
edges, thin coats. Water-based enamel self-levels; do not go back over a wet area.
Overnight.

**Session 4: second colour coat, MDF only.** Light 220 scuff first. Doors and right
side. A third coat if you can see roller texture in low light.

Cure: dry in hours, hard in about a week. Handle parts after a day, build the ladder
after two, hang doors after three, load the slab after a week.

## Room

Paint indoors in the room the cabinets go in, or a garage above 50°F. Doors on
pyramids on sawhorses so both faces go in one session. Store doors flat, stacked
face to face on clean cardboard, never leaning on one edge overnight.
