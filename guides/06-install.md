# 6. Installation

One day with a helper for the tall cabinet. Everything painted, doors off, hinges
set. You need a 4' level, a long straight board or laser level, a stud finder, shims,
the connector screws, and the 4" structural screws.

## Reference lines

1. Find every stud on the rear wall between 5" and 20" off the floor and mark the
   centres with tape above the panelling line so the marks survive.
2. Mark the panelling bottom edge height on the left wall and right wall: 19-15/16.
   This is the top of the slab.
3. Slab top minus 3/4 slab = 19-3/16 is the top of the TV boxes. Mark it on the rear
   wall at both ends and snap or laser a line. Everything is set to this line.
4. Box underside = 19-3/16 minus 14-1/4 = 4-15/16 off the floor. Check the floor along
   the wall with the level. Note the high spot; that is where the legs are shortest.

## Legs

Screw four legs to the underside of each box, 2" in from each corner, front legs set
back 2-1/2 plus 3/4 from the front edge so the kick plate clips to them and lands at
the right recess. On the tall cabinet the front legs go 3/4 back so the flush kick
clips to them. Set every leg to 4-7/8 to start.

## Order

**Tall cabinet first.**
1. Stand it against the left wall and rear wall. Plumb the front edge with the level.
   Adjust the legs until the top is level side to side and front to back, and the box
   underside is at 4-15/16.
2. If the left wall leans, shim between the cabinet side and the wall at the rail
   heights so the cabinet stays plumb. A 1/8 gap at the wall disappears under the
   door's 1/4 end gap.
3. Drive 4" screws through the top rail and bottom rail into two studs each. 4" not
   3": the rail is 1-1/2 solid lumber, so a 3" screw reaches only 3/4 into the stud.
   Pilot
   drill first. Check plumb again after each screw; screws pull a box toward the wall.

**TV box 1.**
1. Set it against the tall cabinet's right side. Adjust legs until the top is on the
   line and level.
2. Clamp it to the tall cabinet with the fronts flush; the box front sits 8-7/16
   behind the tall cabinet's front, so clamp across a straight offcut that references
   the tall cabinet's side, not its front.
3. Drive four 1-1/4 connector screws from inside the box through its left side into
   the tall cabinet's right side: two near the front, two near the rear, 3" from top
   and bottom. Pilot drill. The tall side is 3/4 MDF; screws bite 1/2" into it.
4. Two 4" screws through the rail into studs.

**Boxes 2 and 3.** Same: legs to the line, clamp to the neighbour with fronts and
tops flush, four connector screws through the shared sides, rail to studs. Box 3
should finish 0 to 1/4 short of the right wall. If it is proud of the wall, the wall
measurement in guide 2 was wrong; plane the right side's front edge back or accept a
small door gap change.

## Kick plates

Clip the tall kick to the tall cabinet legs, flush with the door plane. Clip the TV
kick pieces to the box legs, 2-1/2 behind the door plane, joints behind the box
seams. Fit the short return where the two planes meet. Kick plates come off for
cleaning under the unit.

## Doors

Clip every door back on its hinges. Set reveals to 1/8 with a spacer and the hinge
adjustment screws. The tall door's bottom edge lines up with the tall cabinet bottom;
TV door bottoms line up with the box bottoms.

Check: open every door fully. Nothing touches the wall, the tall cabinet, or a
neighbouring door.

Then guide 7, the top slab.
